# Launch docs, 27 Nov 2026: what is now official, and the plan

Source: all 23 pages of yieldfields.fun/docs, every one "Updated 27 Nov 2026" (snapshot:
`docs/source/yieldfields-docs-2026-11-27.txt`). No redactions remain except one line in Truffles
("5% of each day's payout is set aside for ████, classified until day 14").

## 0. Eligibility (read first)
The docs now state, in Quick start, FAQ and Fees and risks: **"Yield Fields isn't available to US persons or in
sanctioned or embargoed countries. By playing you agree to the Terms of Service"**, and "wallets may be screened".
The game can also put an account under review and pause its selling, claiming and exports. If the owner is a US
person this is a decision for the owner to make with the Terms of Service in front of them; nothing in this repo
is advice on that, and nothing here should be used to get around a restriction.

## 2. What the numbers say (`yf/roi.py`, 43 tests)
- **Farmer edge over a market buyer:** 4.0× the tokens at T+30 min, 2.5× at 1 h, 1.6× at 2 h, 1.2× at 3 h, 1.1× at
  4 h, none at 6 h.
- **Exit break-even for the 8 seeds held (2.0 ETH paid, 1.75 ETH of crops):** price must be 1.25× the Farmer
  entry at a 3% sell tax, 1.52× at 20%. Average hand: 1.10× / 1.34×.
- **Pool:** starts with tokens only, so the opening price is set by the pool's range and is **not published**.
  Illustration (open-ended range assumed; depth ≈ 0.8 × starting cap): 1,000 ETH of presale crops, if half go to
  the Farmer, lift a $0.25M start ~21× (→ ~$5M cap), a $1M start ~3.6×, a $5M start ~1.4×. Read the real
  reserves with `poolwatch` at launch.
- **Co-op:** $250k fund → $7,500/day of buys (4.4 ETH): $5,250 to truffles, $2,250 to deeds. With presale dues
  (~$119k) and the full seed: ~$18,600/day. **Per Rare deed: ~$0.7–1.7/day if every staked deed is tended,
  ~$1.4–3.3/day if half are** (after the 10% claim fee). On a ~$1,400 deed that is roughly 1.5–7% a month, paid in
  `$YIELD`, falling 3%/day unless fees refill the fund.
- **Truffles, day 1:** 419k tokens shared over ~1,000 ETH of crops. 8 seeds = 0.18% → ~740 tokens expected
  (57% chance of at least one truffle). That is ~$18 at a $2.5M cap, ~$184 at $25M. A bonus, not a reason to trade.
- **Truffles, later days:** 1 ETH farmed on a day with 200 ETH of network weight earns ~2,000 tokens at pig
  level 1, ~4,150 at level 4. Recycling through the Broker (8.5% rake) pays above a ~$6.8M cap at level 1, ~$3.3M
  at level 4; on a 50-ETH day, above ~$0.8M. **Pig level is the multiplier that deed holders get nearly free.**
- **Pig plan:** 110 Fertilizer → level 9 (2.88×) in 6 days; 135 → level 10 in 7.5 days. Feeds before launch do nothing.
- **Root Cellar:** 32.87 ETH today (0.010 per Rare). Every 10 ETH of launch tax paid by market buyers adds 0.0012 ETH per Rare.

## 3. Plan
**Today (before launch)**
1. Buy the **Truffle Pig (0.01 ETH)**. Without it no crop planted on Saturday can find a truffle.
2. Packs: the conditions set on 25 Nov for adding to the plot are met (Farmer open at launch, buy tax far above
   6.5%). With 1.0 ETH in the wallet that means **up to 3 more Diamond (11 total)**, leaving ~0.24 ETH for the pig,
   gas and Bronze tending packs. Optional; expected cost of each extra pack is the ~8.5% Broker rake.
3. Count the Fertilizer in the Bag. Budget: 2 per seed (16–22) for crops, the rest to the pig.

**Saturday 14:00 UTC**
1. Plant every seed (the first three tend the three deeds). 2 Fertilizer on each, Commons first.
2. Feed the pig 3 Fertilizer (level-1 wait 16 h → 4 h).
3. 14:30 Commons, 15:00 Uncommons, 16:00 Epic: harvest and sell to the Farmer. Expect "price moved" refusals
   (1% guard); re-quote and resend, smaller batches if needed.
4. Run `poolwatch`; follow the ladder (2.0× sell half; 3.5× sell half again; −30% from peak after 1.5× → keep 15%;
   T+3 h and nothing sold and ≥1.2× → sell half). If a crop ripens after the price has run several-fold, sell
   that crop to the Broker.
5. 18:00 UTC: train the pig to level 2 (Normal trainer), feed 6. Then level 3 at ~02:00 Sun (feed 9), level 4 at
   ~14:00 Sun (feed 12), and so on as Fertilizer allows.

**From Sunday**
- Claim Truffle payouts and Co-op Rewards (claim Co-op Rewards in larger, less frequent batches: the 10% fee is
  proportional, so batching only saves gas).
- **Keep the deeds tended: plant 3 seeds of any tier every 5–6 days** (Bronze is enough: 0.005 ETH each, ~92% back).
- Each day before buying more packs, check the Truffles tab ("Pays out today", "Weight today") with
  `truffle_day_ev(..., pig_size=...)`. Recycle through the Broker only when it clears the 8.5% rake.
- Fertilizer: pig leveling is a new sink (135 per pig, ~630 pig-owning wallets possible) against a throttled
  export float. Keep exporting only the free 3/day, and only what the pig and crops do not need.

## 4. Deeds: buy more?
Still no. Co-op Rewards are now quantified at roughly $0.7–3.3 per Rare per day, in `$YIELD`, from a fund that
spends itself down 3% a day unless fees refill it. Root Cellars also gain from launch-day market buyers. That is a
real but small yield on a ~$1,400 deed (floor rose 0.69 → 0.75 ETH after the docs). The three held at a 0.1 ETH
basis already capture it.
