Staking and the Fert Deck
In short:
Stake a Founding Deed, build a Fert Deck on it, and it makes Fertilizer around the clock. Staking is free apart from gas, and a Fert Deck costs 0.01 ETH at launch.
Everything in Yield Fields is subject to change, including these numbers and mechanics. We'll give players ample notice before any change.
Staking
Free apart from gas. Stake any number of deeds in one transaction
Stake and build in the same step
Your deed is held by the staking contract while it's staked. It stays yours
Unstake any time, including while the game is paused
Unstaking destroys the structure on the deed. Any Fertilizer it had made that you hadn't claimed goes into your Bag for you.
Building a Fert Deck
Each staked deed holds 1 structure. The Fert Deck is the first, and more types are planned
It costs 0.01 ETH at launch, shown before you confirm. The fee may change
The fee stays in the deed staking contract and fills the deeds' Root Cellars. See 
The Root Cellar
Structures aren't part of the NFT. The game draws them on your plot
Making Fertilizer
Your Fert Deck makes Fertilizer on its own, around the clock
It makes half a Fertilizer a day for each point of Fertility, from 2.5 a day on a Common to 4.5 a day on a Mythic
Storage holds up to 3 days of production, from 7.5 on a Common to 13.5 on a Mythic. When it's full, the deck stops until you claim
Claimed Fertilizer goes to your in-game 
Bag
. Partial progress carries over
Fert Decks open before farming, so you can stockpile Fertilizer ahead of farming day 1. Check in at least every 3 days to keep your deck running.
