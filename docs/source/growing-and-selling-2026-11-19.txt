Growing and selling
In short:
Plant seeds in your free farm plot, harvest when they're ripe, then sell to the Farmer for $YIELD or the Broker for ETH.
Everything in Yield Fields is subject to change, including these numbers and mechanics. We'll give players ample notice before any change.
Selling fees will be announced before farming opens.
Your farm plot
A grid of slots, free for every wallet. Each slot holds 1 crop at a time
There are no plot expansions, and you don't need a deed to farm
Other players can visit your plot and see what you're growing
Growing
From 1 to 12 hours, with a live countdown in the game. See every crop's grow time in 
Seeds and crops
Rarer crops take longer, and bigger packs grow faster
Fertilizer cuts 25% of the grow time each, up to 2 per crop. Use it from your Bag. See 
Fertilizer
Ripe crops never wilt, so harvest on your own schedule
Harvesting
Harvest 1 crop or everything ripe at once. Harvested crops go to your Bag, ready to sell.
