The Root Cellar
The Root Cellar
In short:
 Every Founding Deed has its own pot of ETH, called its Root Cellar. Fees fill the cellars over time, and a deed's Fertility decides how big its share is. Burn a staked deed to cash out its cellar in ETH.
note
Everything in Yield Fields is subject to change, including these numbers and mechanics. We'll give players ample notice before any change.
“Nobody remembers who dug the cellars under the Founding Deeds. They fill slowly, and rarer land runs deeper.”
In plain terms
A slice of every Fert Deck build fee, and later every $YIELD trade, is set aside in ETH for deeds
Each deed's slice is its Root Cellar, and higher Fertility means a bigger slice
Burning a staked deed is how you collect it
How it fills
Every deed has a cellar,
 staked or not, and its Fertility sets how deep it is. Only a staked deed can be burned to collect it
It fills from
 Fert Deck build fees now, and from part of every $YIELD buy and sell tax once $YIELD launches
No set amount.
 It starts filling from day 1 as deed holders build
Why deeds get stronger over time
Cellars fill
 with every Fert Deck built, and later with every $YIELD trade
Burns shrink the collection.
 Every burned deed is gone for good, so the rest share future fees
Only deeds make Fertilizer,
 and every farmer will want it
Collecting it
Burning a staked deed is the only way to open its cellar, and it pays out in ETH. The same burn also wins a share of the Silo. See 
 The Silo and the Windfall
 for how to burn.
A Root Cellar has no set amount and isn't a price for your deed. It moves with how much is built and traded.

In plain terms
Deed holders pay a small fee when they claim deed rewards, and part of that fee goes into the Silo

The Silo

The Windfall
Burn a staked deed and you win the Windfall, a share of the Silo set by its Fertility. The same burn empties its Root Cellar in ETH.

How the Farmers Co-op works
In short:
 The Farmers Co-op is the farmers' collective pot, and the engine that ties the game together. Every farmer is a member just by selling a harvest. What it does stays under wraps until farming opens.
note
Everything in Yield Fields is subject to change, including these numbers and mechanics. We'll give players ample notice before any change.
“The Farmers Co-op doesn't farm. It keeps the fields fed.”
What feeds it
Co-op Dues
 from every crop sold, at the Farmer or the Broker
A share of the taxes
 on every $YIELD trade, plus the extra that heavy selling pays
Fees from Fert Deck boosts,
 once they arrive
Its balance is shown at the Farmers Co-op in the game.
What we can tell you
It works in the open.
 Its balance is shown in the game, and everything it does happens onchain
It doesn't add to heavy selling.
 Its own trades never count toward the higher sell tax
It works for the fields.
 What it collects goes back into the farm and its rewards. Some of it may never come back out
The Farmers Co-op wakes on day 1 of farming, and it won't start from zero: it gets seeded with ████████ on day 1. Until then, it waits and it fills. Its rules are published before it makes its first move.
How it all connects
1
Deeds make Fertilizer.
 Farmers use it, or buy it from deed holders, to grow faster.
2

FAQ

 Quick answers, with the answer first.
note
Everything in Yield Fields is subject to change, including these numbers and mechanics. We'll give players ample notice before any change.
Do I need a deed to play?
No.
 Farming opens to everyone after Stage One. Deeds let you stake, build a Fert Deck and make Fertilizer. Want one? Founding Deeds trade on 
OpenSea
. See 
 Founding Deeds
.
Does staking cost anything?
No.
 Only gas. Building a Fert Deck costs 0.01 ETH at launch.
Can I sell a staked deed?
No.
 Unstake it first. Unstaking destroys its structure, and any unclaimed Fertilizer goes into your Bag for you.
Can I unstake while the game is paused?
No.
 You can unstake again as soon as it resumes.
Do I earn $YIELD or ETH for staking right now?
Not yet.
 Staked deeds are lined up for deed rewards, ██████████ and the Silo. Additional rewards for deed owners will be launched when the farming game launches.
Does a higher rarity produce more?
Yes.
 Fert Deck output scales with Fertility, from 5 for a Common to 9 for a Mythic.
What happens if I don't claim my Fertilizer?
Your deck stops producing once its storage is full, at 3 days of production.
Can I trade Fertilizer?
Yes.
 Export it to your wallet first. See 
 Fertilizer
.
Does Fertilizer expire?
No.
 Stock up whenever you like.
Do I need $YIELD to play?
No.
 You pay for everything in ETH. $YIELD is only a reward.
Will I make money farming?
Farming isn't a guaranteed profit.
Should I sell to the Farmer or the Broker?
The Farmer if you want to hold $YIELD, since it pays more. The Broker if you want ETH. See 
 Growing and selling

Are the odds fair?
Yes.
 Every pack is tied to a future draw from drand, a public randomness source. The result is fixed before anyone can see it, and anyone can check it.
What stops people from dumping $YIELD?
The sell tax rises during heavy selling, and the extra goes to the Farmers Co-op. At launch, a high buy tax that drops over time stops bots from sniping the opening price.
Does the team hold $YIELD?
The team got 0% of the supply.
