Co-op Rewards
In short:
Staked deeds get Co-op Rewards in $YIELD, from the Farmers Co-op's daily buys, on every day they're tended. Plant 1 seed on your own plot and it tends 1 of your staked deeds for 7 days.
Co-op Rewards start with the game on Sat 28 Nov at 14:00 UTC. You can stake now, and deeds are tended by seeds planted from then on.
Everything in Yield Fields is subject to change, including these numbers and mechanics. We'll give players ample notice before any change.

 on your own plot. It tends 1 of your staked deeds for the day you plant and the 6 days after
3
At 14:00 UTC each day,
 that day's Co-op Rewards are split by Fertility among the deeds tended that day
4
Claim
 whenever you like. 1 claim covers all your deeds
The Farmers Co-op starts buying 6 hours after launch, when the launch tax ends.
Any seed tends, and your least-tended deed goes first
Any seed counts,
 whatever its tier. Planting tends. Harvesting, Fertilizer and selling don't
Fewest days first.
 Each seed goes to your deed with the fewest tended days left. On a tie, the rarer deed goes first
Up to 7 days ahead.
 Once all your deeds are tended that far, more seeds just farm
Your own plot only.
 Seeds planted from another wallet don't tend the staking wallet's deeds, even from a linked wallet
25 slots a plot.
 1 full plot a week tends 25 deeds. A full plot every day tends 175, and twice a day 350
Tended and staked all day means a part of that day
Staked all day.
 A deed counts on a day it's tended and staked from 14:00 to 14:00 UTC by the wallet that tended it
New stakes

Fertility sets the split.
 A tended Mythic gets 1.8× what a tended Common does
Unstaking, selling or burning
 ends a deed's tended days, and it gets nothing for that day
Nobody tended?
 That day's Co-op Rewards roll into the next day
Settled means yours.
 Settled Co-op Rewards are never taken back and never expire
1 claim pays out every deed

 1 claim pays out every deed's settled Co-op Rewards in $YIELD
Every deed listed.
 The claim shows what each deed got, including deeds you've since sold, unstaked or burned
10% claim fee.
 5% goes to the next day's Co-op Rewards for tended deeds, and 5% into the Silo. See

Fertilizer
Fertilizer
In short:
Fertilizer makes crops grow faster, never expires, and only Fert Decks make it. Use it on your own farm, or export it and sell it to farmers on OpenSea.
Making, claiming and exporting Fertilizer are live now. Using it on crops and Truffle Pigs starts with the game on Sat 28 Nov at 14:00 UTC.
Everything in Yield Fields is subject to change, including these numbers and mechanics. We'll give players ample notice before any change.
Cut a crop's grow time
25% faster.
 Each Fertilizer cuts 25% of a crop's total grow time
Up to 2 per crop.
 So a crop can grow in half the time
Instant ripen.
 Use one with less than a quarter of the time left and the crop ripens at once
Used up when applied.
 Until then it keeps in your Bag or wallet
Feeds your Truffle Pig.
 Each feed skips 25% of its current level's wait. See 
Pig Levels
Sourced from deeds.
 Every farmer who wants faster harvests gets it from deed holders
Export it and trade it on OpenSea
1
Claim
 Fertilizer from your Fert Deck. It goes to your Bag.
2
Export
 it from your Bag. This mints it to your wallet as an onchain item.
3
Trade
 it on 
OpenSea
, like any other item.
4
Import
 it back into your Bag when you want to use it in the game. Importing doesn't lift an exporter slowdown (see "Decks slow down, never stop" below).
Export is live now. Items in your wallet can't be used in the game until you import them.
From launch, every item exports the same way as Fertilizer: Seed Packs, Seeds and Crops too. Import an item back into your Bag to plant, open or sell it.
Exported items only move between standard wallets. Smart-contract wallets and wallets upgraded with EIP-7702 can't transfer them, so export to a standard wallet.
Link a second wallet to share one Bag
Before the game begins.
 Linked wallets go live before the game opens on Sat 28 Nov at 14:00 UTC
2 wallets, 1 Bag.
 Link 2 wallets you own and they share one Bag: Fertilizer, Seed Packs, Seeds and Crops
How to link.
 Your first wallet enters the second wallet's address and signs. The second wallet signs to accept, and sharing starts at once
Your share first.
 Either wallet can plant, use Fertilizer, feed its Truffle Pig and open Seed Packs from the shared Bag. It uses its own share first, then its partner's
Selling and exporting
 use each wallet's own share
Still separate.
 Each wallet keeps its own plot, Truffle Pig, deeds, export limit and payouts
Unlinking
 takes effect 72 hours after you ask. Each wallet takes back its own share. After an unlink, linking again waits 7 days
Only link a wallet you control. A linked wallet can use your share of the Bag, and the team will never ask you to link a wallet.
Run more Fert Decks, export more each day
Daily limit.
 Each wallet can export a set amount of Fertilizer every 24 hours
Half your Decks' output.
 The limit is half of what your Fert Decks make in a day, counting every Fert Deck you've run in the last 7 days
Per deck.
 Each Common's Fert Deck adds 1.25 a day and each Mythic's adds 2.25
Whole Fertilizer.
 The limit rounds down, so a single Common, Rare or Epic exports 1 a day and a single Legendary or Mythic 2
Imports add to it.
 Fertilizer you imported in the last 24 hours adds to your limit
No check, no export.
 If the game can't check your limit, export pauses until it can
Decks slow down, never stop
A network-wide slowdown keeps Fertilizer from piling up. Fertilizer used on crops leaves circulation, so only what piles up counts.
Network rate.
 Every hour, if circulation is above 7 days of the network's output, every Fert Deck slows: 70% speed at 10 days, and 50% at 14 days or more
Exporter rate.
 If a wallet exports more than 25% of its Decks' daily output in any 24 hours, its Decks slow: 90% speed at 30%, 70% at 40% and 50% at 50% or more
Your first 25% is free.
 It rounds up to whole Fertilizer, so a single Common, Rare, Epic or Legendary always exports 1 a day at full speed, and a single Mythic 2
When it counts.
 Each export counts from the moment you make it, and importing back doesn't undo it
It recovers on its own
 once those exports are more than 24 hours old. The rate updates every hour
Keeping it never slows you.
 Fertilizer you keep in your Bag never counts toward your exporter rate
Never below half speed.
 A Deck runs at the lower of the 2 rates. Fertilizer you hold is never taken away
Public numbers.
 The circulation number, the target and the current network rate are public. These are launch settings

The Root Cellar
In short:
Every Founding Deed has its own Root Cellar of ETH, filled by fees and sized by its Fertility, and burning a staked deed pays it out. A Root Cellar has no set amount and isn't a price for your deed.
Fert Deck build fees fill the Root Cellars now. $YIELD trading taxes and burns start with the game on Sat 28 Nov at 14:00 UTC.
Everything in Yield Fields is subject to change, including these numbers and mechanics. We'll give players ample notice before any change.
“Nobody remembers who dug the cellars under the Founding Deeds. They fill slowly, and rarer land runs deeper.”
Fees fill it, Fertility sizes it

 staked or not. Higher Fertility means a deeper cellar
Build fees.
 Every Fert Deck build fee goes into the deeds' cellars
$YIELD trades.

NFT royalties.

Fewer deeds over time.

The Silo, Co-op Rewards claims and burns start with the game on Sat 28 Nov at 14:00 UTC. This page explains how they work from then.
Everything in Yield Fields is subject to change, including these numbers and mechanics. We'll give players ample notice before any change.

What's the launch tax?
A tax on $YIELD buys for the first 6 hours of trading.
 It starts at 90% and eases along a curve to 2% at hour 6: about 63% at 1 hour, 41% at 2 hours, 24% at 3 hours and 12% at 4 hours. Sells never pay it. Yes.
 The Farmer pays no launch tax and no buy tax, from the first minute. Farmers get $YIELD for the 6% Co-op Dues while market buyers pay the launch tax. The first Farmer sales are possible from about minute 30, when the first fertilized crops are ready.
What happens during heavy selling?
The sell surge kicks in.

Know the risks before you play
Farming is a game of chance.
 Every seed is random, and on average farming returns a little less than it costs: a seed is worth 99.5% of its pack price on average, and selling costs 6% at the Farmer or 8% at the Broker. Truffles are a bonus on top.
Randomness.
 Seed rarity and truffle finds are random, so results vary widely.
Truffle payouts.
 For the first 30 days the launch seed adds to them, and after that they come from the Co-op's refills alone, so they move with activity in the game.
Token price.
 $YIELD's price can go up or down, and it isn't an investment. Farmer sales, truffle payouts and Co-op Rewards are paid in $YIELD, so their value moves with it.
Values move.
 Founding Deeds, Fertilizer and rewards move with the market, and rates can change up or down. Nothing here is guaranteed.
Liquidity and trading costs.
 The pool starts single-sided ($YIELD only), so early trades move the price more. Sells cost more during heavy selling, and big trades move the price.
Smart contracts.
 Like any onchain game, the contracts could have bugs.
Setting changes and upgrades.
 The team can change settings and pause contracts, and changes take effect at once. The game contracts can be upgraded, including deed staking, which holds the Root Cellars, Co-op Rewards and the Silo. Updates apply to staked deeds too.
Pauses.
 If the game is paused for maintenance or safety, you can still unstake. Burns wait until it's back.
Root Cellars.
 A Root Cellar grows with fees. It has no set amount and isn't guaranteed to reach any amount.
The game server.
 Co-op Rewards and truffle payouts are counted by the game server and claimed with its signature, so they rely on it.
Burning a deed is permanent.
Dependencies.
 The game runs on Robinhood Chain, Uniswap v4, drand and other third-party infrastructure.
Regulation.
 Rules for digital assets and online games vary by country and can change.
Who can play.
 Yield Fields isn't available to US persons or in sanctioned or embargoed countries, and wallets may be screened against sanctions lists. See the 
Terms of Service
 and 
Privacy Policy

Linked wallets.
 A linked wallet can use your share of the Bag. Only link a wallet you control.
Exported items.
 Only standard wallets can move exported items. Smart-contract wallets and wallets upgraded with EIP-7702 can't transfer them.
Who controls settings
Settings are changed by the owner, a 2-of-3 team multisig (Safe)
Changes take effect onchain when they're made, with no timelock, and every one is logged in Patch notes
The base buy and sell taxes can never go above 5%, hard-coded
The launch tax timing is fixed. The Co-op Rewards claim fee is 10% and can never go above 20%, hard-coded
The $YIELD token, pool hook and registry can't be paused, so nobody can halt trading or transfers. Pausing the Farmers Co-op stops its buys. Pausing deed staking stops everything except unstaking
The $YIELD contracts, the Farmers Co-op's included, can't be upgraded. The team can replace the Farmers Co-op contract with a new one
Liquidity can only be added to the pool, never withdrawn. The contract that manages it can be upgraded by the owner
