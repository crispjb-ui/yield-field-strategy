## Analysis

### 1. The event is a conversion with an unknown ratio
10,000 heroes + 14,000 weapons + 7,000 armor + 125,000 materials + Ringbearers + Rings → one collection, size unstated, "concentrate not dilute" → supply **below** 10,000. Your 41 heroes will not become 41 new NFTs. Everything converts to points; points convert to NFTs at a ratio published only in the event docs. Until those docs exist, nothing here can be valued except by the market's guess, which is what the 0.05 ETH hero floor is.

### 2. Your strongest OCH position is the two Ringbearers
Ringbearers are the premium collection and the most likely holders of the "GTD access + keep their art" clause (the text is written to reassure buyers who "paid a premium"). If that reads correctly, each Ringbearer maps to a guaranteed new-collection NFT with preserved art, which is the best outcome available in the event. **Hold both through the event. Do not sell them into Abstract illiquidity (0 sales in 24 h).** Insufficient data to verify the clause applies; the event docs settle it.

### 3. The 41 heroes are the decision
Three paths:
- **Sell now on Abstract.** 0.05 ETH floor, 9 sales/day. Selling 41 is ~4 days of all volume; realistic exit 0.04–0.05 → ~1.8 ETH. Then Abstract illiquidity and the Dec 15 wall are someone else's problem.
- **Hold and play the event.** Points are "onchain and offchain" weighted; heroes with levels/gear likely score above floor heroes. The game-theory framing means an active player with a large stack can choose the path others avoid. This is where your edge is, if you will actually play 7–10 days in late Nov, concurrently with the Yield Fields launch window.
- **Hold and take the safe path.** Burn for points, receive whatever the ratio gives. Zero effort, but the event is designed so the safe path is the worst-paid one.
Recommendation: **do not sell the heroes at 0.05.** The floor already prices the burn as near-worthless; the asymmetric outcome is a ratio better than the market's fear. Reduce only if you will not have time to play the event. If you do sell, sell a third now while any volume exists, not all.

### 4. Weapons
Mythic and legendary weapons are the items most likely to carry disproportionate points (rarity-weighted, "offchain weight"). Hold. Epic and below: hold; the Abstract market for them will be effectively dead within weeks and selling costs more attention than it returns.
